Third-party companies provide advertisements on our websites. These third-party companies use the information of user visits to our website. Whenever these advertisements are displayed to the user, the third-party companies may place their unique cookies on the user's browser to show relevant ads on future visits.

5. Cookies

Cookies are small data files created by a web server, distributed through a web browser, and stored on the user's computer. These help in tracking the online patterns and preferences of the user, and they identify the user as a return visitor.

The users visiting the website may need to accept cookies to get access to all the features. Cookies also help create customization so the user does not need to add every piece of information on every visit.
